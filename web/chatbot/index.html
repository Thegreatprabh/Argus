Connect your AI key — boom, RE starts

ARGUS Chatbot runs entirely in your browser. Paste your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini — ARGUS auto-detects the provider and the best model from the key itself. No sign-up, no server, no middleman.

How privacy works: your key is saved only in this browser's local storage, on this device. It is sent only to its own provider (OpenAI / Anthropic / Google) — never to ARGUS or anyone else. Every visitor of this page uses their own key in their own browser. Nothing is shared between users.
